# Governance

Fail2Test uses maintainer-led governance. The project steward and current maintainers are listed in MAINTAINERS.md. At launch this may be a small team; the project does not claim foundation oversight or a steering committee it does not have.

Maintainers approve ordinary changes within their responsibility. Public interface/schema, security/privacy default, license or substantial architectural changes require a public proposal/RFC and recorded decision, except private vulnerability handling before disclosure. Nonurgent substantial RFCs normally allow at least seven calendar days for comment.

Contributors retain copyright and submit under the published license with DCO sign-off. Sponsorship and commercial priorities are disclosed and do not override review/security obligations. Existing Apache-2.0 releases remain available under their released license.

New maintainers are invited based on sustained useful contributions and judgment, including documentation, support and review. Access is granted incrementally. Release/security permissions require explicit onboarding, 2FA and recovery planning. Inactive or misused access is reviewed and may be removed, with a documented explanation.

The public roadmap summarizes direction; linked issues and milestones track delivery. Maintainers explain scope changes and update direction during monthly reviews while active. Community decisions must be recorded in a public durable location rather than existing only in private chat.

Publication prerequisite: fill actual maintainer identities and confirm this policy with those people before presenting them as responsible.
